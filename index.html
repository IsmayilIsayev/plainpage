Plainpage Reader

The article, and nothing else.

Plainpage Reader is a reading mode for Chrome. Press Alt+R on a news story, blog post or documentation page and read it in a clean view: just the title, the text and the pictures. No ads, sidebars or pop-ups. Your reading never leaves your browser.
